Provenance. Candidate boxes are recovered from the frozen run's rendered overlays and validated twice: the recovered winner matches the v2_boxes stored in samples.jsonl, and re-executing the real synthesised program on these boxes reproduces that same winner. Cases that fail either check are not shown as executable.

What is live. The scores on this page are computed in your browser by a port of the executor, under the frozen configuration (centroid scoring, graded ramps, σ = 0.25, extremal temperature from candidate spacing). Detection and segmentation are not rerun: the mask overlay is the pipeline's real output. Editing an operator therefore changes the selection, not the mask.
